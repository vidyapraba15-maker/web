United States of America

The United States of America is a country located in North America. It is one of the most developed countries in the world. The capital city is Washington D.C. The United States of America (USA) is a federal republic in North America composed of 50 states, a federal district (Washington, D.C.), and several territories. With over 341 million people, it is the world's third-largest country by land area and population. Known for its immense economic, cultural, and military influence, it is a diverse nation with major cities like New York and landmarks such as the Grand Canyon.
